# ManyChat Comment-Trigger Playbook — The Street Architect

**Purpose:** Drive keyword comments on Instagram / TikTok so your ManyChat automation fires.
The automation already works — this doc fixes the *upstream* problem: getting people to comment the keyword in the first place.

**Primary offer:** Zero to BIM — Beginners Guide (free lead magnet)

---

| Platform | ManyChat trigger works? | What to do |

## The formula (every post)

**Hook (first 3 sec) → deliver value → keyword CTA (last 3–5 sec).**
Reinforce the CTA **3 ways**: said out loud, on-screen text overlay, and first line of the caption.

**3 rules that make triggers actually fire:**
1. Say the keyword out loud AND put it on screen the last 3–5 seconds (85% watch muted).
2. First line of every caption = the CTA: `Comment ZEROBIM 👇`
3. Pin a comment with the keyword yourself right after posting — seeds the behavior.

## Hook + Comment-CTA Library

Brand voice: direct, Detroit-rooted, no fluff. None start with "I." Every hook leads with the viewer's reality.

### ⭐ Zero to BIM Guide — primary-offer hooks

1. **"Never touched BIM and terrified you're already behind? This free guide takes you from zero to your first coordinated model."**
   → *"Comment `ZEROBIM` and I'll send the beginners guide straight to your DMs."*
2. **"Everybody at your firm throws around 'BIM' like you're supposed to already know it. Start here — no experience needed."**
   → *"Comment `ZEROBIM` for the free beginners guide."*
3. **"Thrown into a BIM role you were never trained for? Save this before your next coordination meeting."**
   → *"Comment `ZEROBIM` — the guide I wish I had on day one."*
4. **"You don't need a masters degree to break into BIM. You need the right starting point. Here's the free one."**
   → *"Comment `ZEROBIM` and I'll DM it over."*
5. **"Went from laid off to running BIM for the whole firm in 18 months. It started with the basics in this guide."**
   → *"Comment `ZEROBIM` for the same starting point."*

### Pain-point hooks (highest comment rate)

Why they convert: your audience is drowning in Revit and too embarrassed to admit it. A keyword feels anonymous — safer than a public "I don't get it."

6. **"Revit crashing every time you hit a big model? It's not your computer — it's 3 settings nobody told you about."**
   → *"Comment `FIX` for the crash-proof settings sheet."*
7. **"Still modeling families the slow way while everyone pretends they know what they're doing? Here's the shortcut they're hiding."**
   → *"Comment `FAMILY` for the template pack."*

### Curiosity hooks (what they don't know yet)

Why they convert: the gap between "what I know" and "what I'm missing" is unbearable in a fast-moving field.

8. **"There's a Revit shortcut that cuts modeling time in half — and 90% of the industry has never touched it."**
   → *"Comment `SPEED` for the 12-shortcut cheat sheet."*
9. **"AI can now do the Revit task you spend 4 hours a week on. Most architects have no idea it exists yet."**
   → *"Comment `AI` for the exact workflow."*

### Contrarian / bold-claim hooks

Why they convert: a bold claim makes people want in — or want to argue. Both are comments, both trigger the flow.

10. **"Revit certifications are a waste of money. Here's what actually gets you hired in AEC right now."**
    → *"Comment `PATH` for the roadmap."*
11. **"You don't need a masters degree to run BIM at your firm. You need 5 skills — none taught in school."**
    → *"Comment `SKILLS` for the list."*

### Story hooks (Detroit meets the job site — your brand moat)

Why they convert: story lowers the guard, and the keyword lands right when trust peaks.

12. **"Grew up where nobody talked about architecture as a way out. Now I train people into six-figure BIM roles."**
    → *"Comment `PATH` for the free roadmap."*

### List hooks (fast value = fast comment)

Why they convert: a numbered promise is a clear contract — friction drops to zero.

13. **"5 Revit habits quietly killing your model — number 3 is why your firm's files keep corrupting."**
    → *"Comment `HABITS` for the full breakdown."*
14. **"7 free tools every accidental BIM manager needs. Grab the whole kit."**
    → *"Comment `KIT` and I'll DM all 7."*

## Weekly workflow

1. Pick 1 offer to push that week (default: **Zero to BIM / `ZEROBIM`**).
2. Post 3–5 shorts using hooks above, all driving the same keyword.
3. Pin the keyword comment on each post immediately.
4. Check ManyChat: which keyword pulled the most opt-ins? Double down next week.
